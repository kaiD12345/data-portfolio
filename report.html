2025 sales performance: short analysis report

Problem solved: the owner needs to know where revenue comes from and where to focus next year.

All data on this page is fictional, generated for demonstration. It does not come from any employer or client.

Summary

The five branches generated RWF 57.9M in 2025 at a gross margin of 28.2%. Kigali Central contributed the most revenue (28%). Groceries is the largest category (45% of revenue). Sales peaked in June and were lowest in November, then rebounded in December.

Findings

1. Branch performance differs mainly in volume. Margins are similar across branches (roughly 28-29%), so the gap between branches comes from how much each one sells rather than how profitably.

2. Seasonality matters. Revenue climbed from 4.2M in January to 5.5M in June, fell to 4.1M in November, and December was 23% above November.

3. Category mix is concentrated. Groceries and Household together account for 70% of revenue, so stock availability in those two categories has the largest effect on results.

Suggested next steps

Review stock and staffing for the weaker months, test promotions for the lowest-revenue branches, and track margin by product within Groceries to confirm which items drive profit.

Method and limits

Monthly totals by branch and category (240 records), summed with Python. Gross margin = (revenue - cost) / revenue. The data is fictional and monthly, so this report cannot show product-level or customer-level patterns, and it does not test whether differences are statistically significant.
