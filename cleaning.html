Survey data cleaning: before and after

Problem solved: a program's survey export had duplicates, mixed spellings and formats, and impossible values, so it could not be analyzed or reported on reliably.

All data on this page is fictional, generated for demonstration. It does not come from any employer or client.

Rows312 → 300
District spellings24 → 6
Duplicates removed12

What was wrong, and the rule applied

IssueRuleRecords affected
Duplicate respondentsKeep first record per respondent ID12
District typed as "GASABO ", "gasabo", etc.Trim spaces, standardize capitalization24 spellings → 6
Gender: F, female, M, blankMap to Female / Male; blanks left missing, not guessed18 missing
Attends school: Y, yes, YES, NMap to Yes / No; blanks left missing15 missing
Three date formatsConvert all to YYYY-MM-DD0 unparsed
Ages like 0, 150, 999, "sixteen"Valid range 10-19; outside range set to missing, flagged not invented4 out of range, 2 text values
Scores like 540, -20, "N/A"Valid range 0-100; others set to missing7 out of range, 2 non-numeric

Principle: nothing is guessed. Invalid values become missing and are counted in a log, so the analyst knows exactly what was changed.
